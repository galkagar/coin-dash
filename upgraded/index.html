Coin Dash

Two players race for yellow coins. Hitting a red drone puts a random Drone Curse on you (slow, freeze, shrink, runaway coin, darkness, chasing drones, or reversed controls). Watch out for ice and a collapsing floor, and shove your rival. More drones join as time passes and as coins are collected. Most coins in 2 minutes wins!

P1: WASD · P2: arrow keys · Restart: R
